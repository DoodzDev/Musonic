What you get

Playback

  • Synced lyrics

    Line by line in the player. Tap a line to jump to it.

  • A queue you control

    Shuffle, repeat, autoplay, reorder, and restored where you left it.

  • Shuffle Magic

    Your songs, with related tracks found online in between.

Discovery

  • A home that knows you

    Recently played, picks for you and artists to discover.

  • Magic Mixes

    One mix per genre, with about 3 discoveries in 10.

  • One search

    Your library and the online catalogue, with a top result.

Offline and data

  • Downloads

    Playlists, albums or single tracks, ready with no signal.

  • Wi-Fi first

    Downloads wait for Wi-Fi; streaming quality is set per network.

  • Private by design

    No account, no analytics. Only artist and track names leave your phone, for photos and lyrics.

Get started

  1. 1

    Run a music server

    Install Navidrome, or use any Subsonic-compatible server you already have.

  2. 2

    Install Musonic

    Download the APK on Android, or sideload the IPA on iPhone.

  3. 3

    Sign in to your server

    Enter its address, your username and password. Your library is there.
