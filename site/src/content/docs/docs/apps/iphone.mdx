There is **no Musonic app for iPhone or iPad**, and none is planned. On iOS, you listen with the Musonic web player in Safari or Chrome, and you add it to your Home Screen so it opens like an app, with its own icon. This page takes you through it step by step, then tells you honestly what works less well than on Android.

**What you need**

- An iPhone or iPad with a recent iOS or iPadOS.
- The **address** of your server, your **username** and your **password**.
- A server address starting with **`https://`** (see the caution below).

<Aside type="caution" title="Your server needs an https:// address">
The web player at `musonic.doodz.dev/app/` is an `https://` page, and Safari blocks it from calling a server in `http://`. If your server is only reachable at an address like `http://192.168.1.20:4533`, ask its administrator to install the web player on the server (it is then at `http://192.168.1.20:4533/app/`), or to give the server an `https://` address.
</Aside>

## Add Musonic to your Home Screen

The pictures below are drawings of the iOS screens: the buttons move a little between iOS versions, so look for the same icon and the same words.

<Tabs>
<TabItem label="Safari">

<Steps>

1. **Open Safari and go to `musonic.doodz.dev/app`.**

   Or your server's own copy, `https://<your server>/app/`. The Musonic sign-in screen appears. You do not need to sign in yet.

2. **Tap the Share button.**

   It is a square with an arrow pointing up. On iOS 26, tap **⋯** at the bottom right of the address bar first, then **Share**. On iOS 18 and earlier, it is directly in the toolbar at the bottom of the screen (at the top right on an iPad).

   <img class="diagram" src="/assets/img/docs/diagrams/iphone-safari-01-share.en.svg" alt="Drawing of Safari on an iPhone showing the Musonic sign-in page, with the Share button (a square with an arrow pointing up) circled in the bottom toolbar." />

3. **Tap "Add to Home Screen".**

   It is in the list under the row of apps. If you do not see it, scroll down or tap **View More**.

   <img class="diagram" src="/assets/img/docs/diagrams/iphone-safari-02-add-to-home.en.svg" alt="Drawing of the iOS share sheet open over the page, with the line 'Add to Home Screen' and its plus-in-a-square icon highlighted." />

4. **Tap "Add".**

   The name is already "Musonic": you can change it. If you see **Open as Web App**, leave it on. Tap **Add** at the top right.

   <img class="diagram" src="/assets/img/docs/diagrams/iphone-safari-03-add.en.svg" alt="Drawing of the 'Add to Home Screen' screen: the Musonic icon, the name Musonic, the address musonic.doodz.dev, the 'Open as Web App' switch turned on, and the Add button highlighted at the top right." />

5. **Open Musonic from its icon on your Home Screen.**

   It opens in its own window, without Safari's address bar.

   <img class="diagram" src="/assets/img/docs/diagrams/iphone-04-home-screen.en.svg" alt="Drawing of an iPhone Home Screen with the black Musonic icon among other app icons, highlighted, with the name Musonic under it." />

</Steps>

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Chrome">

Chrome on iOS can also add a page to the Home Screen on recent versions of iOS (16.4 and later).

<Steps>

1. **Open Chrome and go to `musonic.doodz.dev/app`.**

2. **Tap the Share button in the address bar.**

   It is the square with an arrow pointing up, at the right of the address. If it is not there, tap **⋯** (bottom right) and then **Share**.

   <img class="diagram" src="/assets/img/docs/diagrams/iphone-chrome-01-share.en.svg" alt="Drawing of Chrome on an iPhone showing the Musonic sign-in page, with the Share button circled at the right end of the address bar." />

3. **Tap "Add to Home Screen".**

   Scroll the list or tap **View More** if you do not see it.

4. **Tap "Add".**

5. **Open Musonic from its icon on your Home Screen.**

</Steps>

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Sign in the first time

<Steps>

1. **Tap the Musonic icon on your Home Screen.**

2. **Type your server's address, your username and your password.**

   For example `https://music.example.com`. Musonic adds `https://` if you leave it out.

3. **Tick "Remember me" if this iPhone is yours.**

   Confirm with **Turn on**. Without it, you will have to sign in again each time iOS closes the app.

4. **Tap "Sign in".**

</Steps>

The app on your Home Screen does not share its sign-in with Safari: even if you were signed in in Safari, sign in once more in the app. The sign-in screen, the errors and what happens to your password are explained in [The web player](/docs/apps/web/#sign-in).

## What works less well on iPhone

The web player is made for computers first. On iOS, a web page also has less freedom than an app. Here is what to expect.

| Topic | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Screen layout** | The layout is a computer one. On an iPhone held upright, the screen is too narrow (under about 760 px) and the side menu with **Home**, **Search** and **Library** is hidden. Turn the iPhone sideways: on most recent iPhones, the menu then appears. On an iPad, the menu is shown in landscape and, on most models, upright too. |
| **Playback in the background** | iOS can pause or close a web page that is in the background. Playback usually goes on with the screen locked, but it may stop after a long time, and the next track may not start. This has not been tested on every iOS version. |
| **Crossfade** | Leave **Crossfade between tracks** on **Off** on an iPhone. When it is on, the sound goes through the browser's audio engine (Web Audio), which iOS pauses more readily in the background. |
| **Volume** | iOS does not let a web page change the volume: use the iPhone's buttons. The volume slider may have no effect. |
| **Offline** | No offline listening: the web player streams everything and needs the connection to your server. Downloads exist only in the [Android app](/docs/apps/android/). |
| **Car** | No CarPlay. Bluetooth in the car works like for any other sound from the iPhone. |
| **Mini player, full screen** | The floating mini player and the browser full-screen button are for computers: they do not work on iPhone. |
| **Sleep timer** | It works while the page runs. If iOS has paused the page, it may go off late. |
| **Updates** | Automatic: it is a web page. You get the latest version each time it loads, with nothing to install. A copy hosted on your server follows the updates its administrator installs. |

What works the same as on a computer: your library, search, playlists, likes, lyrics, the queue, podcasts, imports and Musonic Connect, with the same account as on your other devices.

## Remove it

Touch and hold the Musonic icon, tap **Remove App**, then **Delete from Home Screen** (or **Delete Bookmark**). Your playlists and likes are on your server: nothing is lost.
