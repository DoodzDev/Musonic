This page shows how to listen to podcasts in Musonic: find a show, follow it, play its episodes and pick up where you stopped, on any of your devices.

**What you need**

- An account on a **Musonic Server** (podcasts are not available with Navidrome or other servers).
- The Android app or the web player.

Shows are read by your server from their public RSS feed; the episode audio also goes through your server, so the podcast host never sees you.

## Find a show

<Tabs syncKey="client">
<TabItem label="Android">

<Steps>

1. **Open the Search tab and type the name of a show, or of a person.**

2. **Tap the "Podcasts" filter.**

   You get the shows found under **Podcasts**, each with its cover, its name and its author, then **Episodes with this person** when the server can search by person (see below).

   <img class="shot-phone" src="/assets/img/docs/apps-podcasts/01-search.en.webp" alt="The app's Search for Radiolab with the Podcasts filter selected, and the Podcasts list: each show with its cover, its name and its author." />

3. **Tap a show to open its page.**

   An episode found by person opens its show, scrolled to that episode, and plays it.

</Steps>

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Web">

<Steps>

1. **Open Search and type the name of a show, or of a person.**

   Shows also appear under **All**.

2. **Click the "Podcasts" filter.**

   <img class="shot-wide" src="/assets/img/docs/apps-podcasts/02-web-search.en.webp" alt="Musonic Web search for France Inter with the Podcasts filter selected, and the Podcasts section with show cards, each with its cover, name and author." />

3. **Click a show to open its page.**

</Steps>

You can also **paste the address of an RSS feed** (starting with `https://`) in the search field: an **RSS feed** card, **Open this feed**, opens that show.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

**Where results come from.** Shows are searched in the Apple Podcasts (iTunes) directory, in the country of your language (France for French, United States otherwise in the app). If the administrator added a **Podcast Index** key in the console, search also finds the shows that directory misses, and the episodes where a person (host or guest) speaks. Without it, **Episodes with this person** does not show.

## Follow a show

<Steps>

1. **On the show's page, tap or click "Follow".**

   The button sits under the cover, the show's name, its author and its number of episodes, above the description. It becomes **Following**. The show is now in your Library.

   <img class="shot-phone" src="/assets/img/docs/apps-podcasts/03-show-page.en.webp" alt="A podcast's page in the app: the cover, the name The Daily, the author, 70 episodes, the Follow button and the description." />

2. **Find it later in Library → Podcasts.**

   <img class="shot-phone" src="/assets/img/docs/apps-podcasts/04-library-podcasts.en.webp" alt="The app's Library on the Podcasts tab, listing the four shows followed with their author." />

</Steps>

You can open a show and play its episodes without following it. Your server reads the feeds of followed shows **every hour**: new episodes appear on the show's page by themselves. Musonic does not send notifications for new episodes.

## Listen to an episode

**Tap or click an episode** on the show's page. Episodes are listed newest first, each with its date and its length, or **"12 min left"** when you started it, or **Played** once you listened past 95 %.

<Tabs syncKey="client">
<TabItem label="Android">

In the full player, an episode has:

- **−15** and **+30**, on each side of the controls (left of Previous, right of Next): back 15 seconds, forward 30 seconds;
- the **speed** button in the bottom row, in place of Shuffle and Repeat. It shows the current speed; each tap moves to the next one: 1×, 1.25×, 1.5×, 1.75×, 2×, 0.75×.

<img class="shot-phone" src="/assets/img/docs/apps-podcasts/05-episode-player.en.webp" alt="The app's full player playing a podcast episode: −15 and +30 on each side of the controls, and the speed button showing 1.5× in the bottom row." />

Podcasts are also in **Android Auto**: **Library → Podcasts** lists the shows you follow, then their episodes (see [Android Auto](/docs/apps/android-auto/)).

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Web">

In place of shuffle and repeat, the player bar shows **−15** and **+30** around the controls, and the **speed** button (it shows **1×** at first) left of −15, with the same steps (1×, 1.25×, 1.5×, 1.75×, 2×, 0.75×). The speed is remembered by the browser. On a show's page, **Play** starts the newest episode you have not listened to yet.

<img class="shot-wide" src="/assets/img/docs/apps-podcasts/06-web-episode.en.webp" alt="Musonic Web's player bar playing an episode, with the −15 and +30 buttons and the speed control." />

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

**Resume where you stopped.** Your place in each episode is saved by your server every few seconds and at pause, for your account. Start the episode again on your phone, in the browser or in the car: it goes on from there. An episode marked **Played** starts again from the beginning.

**Transcripts.** When the show publishes a transcript, the **Lyrics** button shows it, line by line, like synced lyrics. An episode never shows song lyrics.

**No crossfade** between episodes, even when crossfade is on.

## Stop following a show

<Steps>

1. **Open the show's page (Library → Podcasts).**
2. **Tap or click "Following".**

   The show leaves your Library. Its page can still be found by search.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note" title="Not available for podcasts">
Episodes cannot be downloaded for offline listening, and they are not added to the queue with your songs: an episode plays on its own, from where you stopped.
</Aside>
