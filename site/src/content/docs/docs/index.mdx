This documentation explains how to use the Musonic apps, how to run your own Musonic Server, and how everything works in detail. Pick the situation that matches yours below: each one leads to the right page.

## Where to start

### Someone gave me access

A friend or a family member runs a Musonic Server and gave you an account. You only need an app and the details they sent you (the server's address, your username and your password).

  <LinkCard title="On Android" href="/docs/apps/android/" description="Install the Musonic app on your Android phone, step by step." />
  <LinkCard title="On iPhone or iPad" href="/docs/apps/iphone/" description="Use the web player and add it to your home screen." />
  <LinkCard title="On a computer" href="/docs/apps/web/" description="Listen in your browser, nothing to install." />
</CardGrid>

### I want my own server

You have music files and want to listen to them everywhere, for you and the people close to you.
