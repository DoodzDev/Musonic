## What you may do

- **Use it**, for free: install the apps, run your own Musonic Server.
- **Self-host it for the people close to you**: your family and your friends can have accounts on your server.
- **Read, study and change the code**, and build it yourself.
- **Share it**, as is or changed, as long as it stays non-commercial and you:
  - give credit (keep my name and a link to the project, and say what you changed);
  - pass the licence on: a link to it, or a copy of it. For the server, also keep the `Required Notice:` line of its `LICENSE` file.

Non-profit uses are explicitly allowed by the server's licence: personal study, hobby projects, private entertainment, and use by charities, schools, public research or government institutions.

## What you may not do

- **Sell** Musonic, an app or a server built from its code, or copies of it.
- **Offer it as a paid service**, for example paid hosting of Musonic Server or paid accounts on it.
- **Use it for any commercial purpose**: inside a product you sell, or to make money in any other way.
- **Suggest that I endorse** your version or your use of it.

If you have a use in mind and are not sure it is non-commercial, ask me first in a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/DoodzDev/Musonic/issues/new/choose).

## Not open source, and why

Musonic's source code is public, but Musonic is **not open source** in the sense of the Open Source Initiative (OSI). OSI-approved licences, such as MIT, GPL or AGPL, must allow anyone to use the software for any purpose, including selling it. I do not want someone to sell what I make, so I chose licences that keep it free for everyone while forbidding commercial use:

- **PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0** for the server, because it is written for software;
- **CC BY-NC 4.0** for the apps and the rest.

One consequence: Musonic cannot be listed in catalogues that only accept free-software licences, such as F-Droid.

## No warranty

Both licences give Musonic "as is", without any warranty: as far as the law allows, I am not liable for damage caused by using it. Keep backups of your music and of the server's data folder (see [Backups and restore](/docs/admin/backups/)).

## Your music is not covered

These licences only cover Musonic itself. Your music files, and the services you connect to your server, keep their own rights and terms: use only sources you have the right to use.
