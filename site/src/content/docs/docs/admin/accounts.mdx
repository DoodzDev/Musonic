This page shows the server's administrator how to give access to family and friends, and how to manage their accounts afterwards. Everything is done in the [administration console](/docs/admin/console/), in **Accounts**.

**What you need**

- An administrator account and the console open.
- A way to send a link to the person (a message, an e-mail).

## Roles: administrator and user

| | User | Administrator |
|---|---|---|
| Listens in the apps (Musonic, Musonic Web, Subsonic clients) | Yes | Yes, exactly the same features |
| Opens the administration console | No | Yes |
| Subject to rights and quotas | Yes | No, never limited |

The server always keeps **at least one active administrator**: the last one cannot be made a user, suspended or deleted.

<Aside type="note">
The very first administrator is created on the command line, when the server is installed:

```sh
docker compose exec musonic-server musonic-server user add <name> --admin
```

`<name>` is the account's name. The command asks for the password (8 characters at least). Everyone else comes in by invitation.
</Aside>

## Invite someone

<Steps>

1. **In the console, open "Accounts" and click "Invite someone".**

   <img class="shot-wide" src="/assets/img/docs/admin-accounts/01-invite-button.en.webp" alt="The Accounts screen with the Invite someone button highlighted at the top right" />

2. **Write a note, choose the role and how long the link is valid.**

   The note is for you only, to find the invitation in the list (for example "My cousin Lea"). It is not the name of the account.

   <img class="shot-wide" src="/assets/img/docs/admin-accounts/02-invite-form.en.webp" alt="The Invite someone window filled in: the note My cousin Lea, the role User selected, and the validity 7 d selected" />

3. **Click "Create the invitation".**

4. **Click "Copy" and send the link to the person.**

   The link is shown **only now**. If you close the window without copying it, cancel the invitation and create another one.

   <img class="shot-wide" src="/assets/img/docs/admin-accounts/03-invite-link.en.webp" alt="The Invite someone window showing the link to send, with the Copy button" />

5. **The person opens the link, chooses a name and a password, then taps "Create my account".**

   <img class="shot-phone" src="/assets/img/docs/admin-accounts/04-invite-page.en.webp" alt="The invitation page on a phone: Welcome to Musonic, the fields Name, Password and Confirm the password, and the Create my account button" />

6. **The page then shows the server's address to add in the app.** The person signs in with the name and password just chosen.

   <img class="shot-phone" src="/assets/img/docs/admin-accounts/05-invite-ready.en.webp" alt="The page Your account is ready on a phone, with the server address to add in the Musonic app" />

</Steps>

### Details on invitations

- **Validity**: 1 hour, 24 hours, 7 days (the default) or 30 days, or a custom length from **1 minute to 1 year**. The window shows the end date.
- **Single use**: once an account is created with it, the link no longer works. Its line in **Invitations** then says "Used by …".
- **Role**: an invitation can create an administrator directly. Choose "User" unless you really want to share the console.
- **Cancel**: in **Invitations**, a pending invitation can be cancelled. The link then says "The administrator cancelled this link."
- **Name taken**: if the person chooses a name already used, the page asks for another one.
- **Password**: 8 characters at least, typed twice.
- What the person sees on an unusable link: "This link was used already", "This link expired. Ask the administrator for a new one.", "The administrator cancelled this link." or "This link does not exist. Check it is complete."
- Only a fingerprint (SHA-256 hash) of the link is stored on the server, never the link itself: that is why it cannot be shown again.

## Change the role or suspend an account

<Steps>

1. **In "Accounts", click the account.**

2. **Under "Role", click "User" or "Administrator".** It is saved at once.

3. **To suspend it, turn "Active account" off.**

   <img class="shot-wide" src="/assets/img/docs/admin-accounts/06-account-page.en.webp" alt="The page of the account lea: the Role choice on User, the Active account switch, the Sign out everywhere button, the Rights and the Quotas" />

</Steps>

A suspended account can no longer sign in (the console tells it "This account is suspended: ask the server's administrator") and its sessions end. **Nothing is deleted**: turn "Active account" back on to restore it.

## Rights and quotas

Rights and quotas apply to **users** only. On an administrator's page, the console says "Administrators are never limited."

### Rights

Four switches, all **on** by default:

| Right | When it is off |
|---|---|
| Import (first switch) | Online songs the person likes or puts in a playlist stay online; they are not imported as files. |
| See the online catalog | Search, pages and playback stay within the library. Playlist imports only look in the library. |
| Download files | Direct downloads of files from the apps are refused. Listening still works. |
| Public playlists | The person's playlists cannot be made public (seen by the other accounts). |

### Quotas

All quotas are **unlimited** by default. For each one, pick a preset, "Custom…" for your own number, or "Unlimited" ("No limit" for the bit rate).

| Quota | Presets | What it counts |
|---|---|---|
| Imports per day | 10, 50 | Imports asked by the person since midnight (UTC). |
| Imports per month | 100, 500 | Imports asked since the 1st of the month (UTC). |
| Largest size of their imports | 1 GB, 5 GB | The total size of the files already imported for the person. |
| Highest playback bit rate | 320, 192, 128 kbit/s | The person's streams are made smaller above it (needs ffmpeg). |

- When a quota is reached, the next songs the person likes stay online and are not queued for import. Nothing is deleted.
- The page shows "This month: N imports." under the quotas, and the **Accounts** list shows imports this month against the monthly quota.
- The account's bit rate limit and the server's **Highest bit rate by default** (Playback screen) both apply: **the lower one wins**.
- Changes are saved as you make them ("Saved" appears).

<img class="shot-wide" src="/assets/img/docs/admin-accounts/07-rights-quotas.en.webp" alt="The Rights and Quotas cards of a user: four switches, then imports per day set to 10, imports per month to 100, largest size to 5 GB and highest bit rate to 192 kbit/s" />

## Reset a password

You never see or type a user's password. You send a link, and the person chooses a new one.

<Steps>

1. **Open the account and click "Password reset link".**

2. **Choose how long the link is valid, and whether to sign the person's devices out.**

   The default is 24 hours. "Sign their devices out" revokes the device keys once the new password is chosen.

   <img class="shot-wide" src="/assets/img/docs/admin-accounts/08-reset-form.en.webp" alt="The window Reset lea's password: the validity 24 h selected and the switch Sign their devices out" />

3. **Click "Create the link", then "Copy", and send it to the person.** Like invitations, it is shown only once.

4. **The person opens the link, types the new password twice and confirms.**

   <img class="shot-phone" src="/assets/img/docs/admin-accounts/09-reset-page.en.webp" alt="The New password page on a phone, for the account lea, with the fields Password and Confirm the password" />

</Steps>

- The link is **single use** and valid from 1 minute to 1 year.
- Creating a new link for an account cancels its earlier unused ones.
- Links not used yet are listed in **Accounts → Password resets in progress**, where they can be cancelled.
- A new password always ends the account's other **sessions** (Musonic Web, console). With "Sign their devices out", its **device keys** are revoked too, and the apps must sign in again.
- To change **your own** password, do the same on your own account page: create the link and open it yourself. Musonic Web and the app have no password field of their own.

## Devices and API keys

The Musonic apps sign in once with the password, then get **a key of their own per device** (it starts with `msk_`). The password itself is not kept on the phone.

- **One account's devices**: on its page, under **Devices**, with the date each key was created and last used.
- **Every device**: **Devices and security** in the menu.
- **Revoke** signs that device out at once. To come back, it signs in again with the password.
- **Lost phone**: revoke its key. To be safe, also send a password reset link.

Subsonic clients that send the password with each request have no key: they are not listed. To lock them out, change the password or suspend the account.

## Sessions and "Sign out everywhere"

- A sign-in in Musonic Web or the console creates a **session** that lasts **30 days** and is extended each time it is used.
- **Sign out everywhere**, on an account's page, ends all its sessions **and** revokes all its device keys: every app must sign in again.
- Suspending an account also ends its sessions.

## Delete an account

<Steps>

1. **Open the account and click "Delete".**

2. **Type the account's name in the box.**

   <img class="shot-wide" src="/assets/img/docs/admin-accounts/10-delete.en.webp" alt="The window Delete lea's account? with the box Type lea to confirm and the Delete for good button" />

3. **Click "Delete for good".**

</Steps>

**What goes**: its likes and ratings, playlists, plays, play queue, podcast subscriptions and listening positions, playlist imports, rights and quotas, sessions and devices. **What stays**: every song of the library, including the files imported because this person liked them. Its imports and invitations keep their line, without the name.

<Aside type="danger">
Deleting cannot be undone. If you only want to stop someone signing in, suspend the account instead. If you deleted by mistake, the only way back is a [backup of the database](/docs/admin/backups/) from before.
</Aside>

You cannot delete your own account from the console, nor the last administrator.
